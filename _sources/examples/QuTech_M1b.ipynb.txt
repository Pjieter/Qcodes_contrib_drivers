    "## Important Notes"
   ]
  },
  {
   "cell_type": "markdown",
   "id": "a408ae6e",
   "metadata": {},
   "source": [
    "1. **Virtual Driver**: This driver does not communicate with the hardware. Always ensure the physical M1b settings match the driver settings.\n",
    "\n",
    "2. **Gain Settings**: Choose gain to avoid saturation (±10V output limit) while maintaining good signal-to-noise ratio.\n",
    "\n",
    "3. **Bandwidth**: Higher gains have lower bandwidth. Check M1b documentation for bandwidth specifications.\n",
    "\n",
    "4. **Input Protection**: The M1b has input protection, but avoid exceeding specified current ranges.\n",
    "\n",
    "5. **Reference**: Use external ref-in connected to a cold ground for lowest noise in cryogenic measurements.\n",
    "\n",
    "6. **AC Coupling**: When using x100ac postgain, the AC coupling has a cutoff frequency - check documentation for your measurement frequency range."
   ]
